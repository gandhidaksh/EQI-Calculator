Effluent Quality Index Calculator

Enter the seven quality parameters at a plant's inlet (raw sewage) and outlet (treated effluent). The tool grades each point on the 0–500 EQI scale and shows how far the water improved through treatment.

EQI scale

EQI ≤ 50 — meets the NGT limit EQI ≤ 100 — meets the CPCB limit EQI > 100 — non-compliant
How the score is computed

Each raw reading is placed in its quality band and interpolated into a 0–500 sub-index. Most KPIs — and the alkaline side of pH — use Equation 3.1:

SII= LI+ (UI − LI) × (CI − CL) (CU − CL) (3.1)

Two cases are handled differently:

  • pH, acidic range — Equation 3.2. pH is the only KPI that can be bad in either direction. Below 7.0, effluent quality worsens as pH falls, so the interpolation runs in reverse — distance is measured down from the upper (neutral) bound:
    SII= LI+ (UI − LI) × (CU − CI) (CU − CL) (3.2)
    Since pH cannot be “removed”, its change is reported as a shift toward or away from neutral rather than a removal %.
  • FC — Equation 3.3. Faecal coliform in sewage and effluent varies over a large exponential range of multiple orders (the scale runs 1 to 100,000 MPN/100 mL), so the SI score for FC is computed on a logarithmic scale:
    SII= LI+ (UI − LI) × (log CI − log CL) (log CU − log CL) (3.3)
    For the same reason, FC reduction is reported as log removal rather than a percentage:
    log removal= log10 Cinlet Coutlet
    A 2 log removal means a 100-fold reduction, a 3 log removal a 1000-fold one; expressing this as “99%” versus “99.9%” would obscure a full order of magnitude.
Where: SII — sub-index score of a KPI CI — concentration of the KPI CL, CU — lower and upper bounds of the KPI concentration range LI, UI — lower and upper score bounds of the category CI fell in

Combining the seven sub-indices:

  • Bands and limits are taken from the reference table derived from the norms of the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and the National Green Tribunal (NGT).
  • A parameter complies when its sub-index is ≤ 100, i.e. it is within the CPCB limit. A sub-index of ≤ 50 means it also meets the stricter NGT limit. Anything above 100 (Moderate or worse) is non-compliant.
  • If every parameter complies, the overall EQI is the maximum of the seven sub-indices — the plant is graded on its worst-performing parameter.
  • If any parameter fails, the overall EQI is the weighted mean of the non-compliant parameters only — so a serious breach cannot be diluted by good numbers elsewhere.
